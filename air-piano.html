How to Play

  1. Allow camera access when prompted.
  2. Place your hand in front of the camera.
  3. Move your index finger over the virtual piano below.
  4. Enter a key's zone to play its note.
  5. Move away and back to play the note again.

💡 Good lighting and keeping your whole hand visible in frame will greatly improve tracking accuracy.
